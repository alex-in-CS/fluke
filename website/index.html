An open-source copilot that hears both sides of your calls and answers with the model and keys you choose.

Download Fluke View source

For Windows 10 and 11 / MIT licensed

01 Listen

Two streams.
No guesswork.

Your mic is You. System audio is Them. Fluke transcribes each one on its own, so every line is attributed exactly, with no virtual audio cable and no speaker guessing.

Deepgram live streaming, or any Whisper-compatible endpoint, local servers included.

02 Answer

It speaks up
when they ask.

Fluke notices when the other side asks you something and drafts the answer as soon as they stop talking. The reply streams in word by word, grounded in the conversation so far.

Or press Ctrl Shift Enter to ask about what is happening right now.

03 See

Ask, and it
looks too.

Attach a screenshot of the screen you are on to any question. Slides, spreadsheets, code: Fluke reads them in context, and only when you ask.

04 Remember

Starts where the
last meeting ended.

When a session ends, Fluke writes notes: decisions, action items, open questions. The next meeting in that profile begins with them already in mind.

  • Meeting notes
  • Profile instructions
  • Linked PDF, DOCX, Markdown

Linked files stay linked. Edit one on disk and the next answer reads the new version.

05 Your keys

Any model.
Your account.

Plug in the providers you already pay for, or run fully local. Keys live in your operating system's keychain, read only by the native core. The interface never sees them.

  • Anthropic
  • OpenAI
  • Gemini
  • Groq
  • OpenRouter
  • DeepSeek
  • xAI
  • Mistral
  • Ollama
  • LM Studio
  • Deepgram
  • Any OpenAI-compatible API

06 Private by design

Nothing sits between you and your model.

No Fluke servers
Requests go straight from your machine to the provider you picked.
No telemetry
No analytics, no tracking, no account to create.
History stays local
Transcripts, answers and notes live in a SQLite file on your disk. Export any session to Markdown.
Native and light
A Rust core on the system WebView. The installer is around 10 MB.

Optional: hide the overlay from screen sharing, so your private notes stay off the shared screen.

07 Hotkeys

One keystroke away.

  1. CtrlShiftEnterAnswer what is happening now
  2. CtrlShiftLStart or stop listening
  3. CtrlShiftKType a question
  4. CtrlShiftSpaceShow or hide the overlay
  5. CtrlShiftXClick through it

Every shortcut is global and configurable.
